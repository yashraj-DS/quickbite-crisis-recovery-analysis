06 — Recovery Roadmap

Six actions, ranked by what the data actually says

The diagnosis changes the priority order — trust comes before (or alongside) operations.

01 / PRIMARY
Run a direct trust campaign — don't wait on delivery fixes
On-time orders get rated almost as badly as late ones. Faster delivery alone won't move ratings. Needs public safety certification, transparent incident reporting, visible accountability.
TRUST
02
Fix delivery at the platform level, not city-by-city
The slowdown is uniform across every city (39.5→60min everywhere) — this is a capacity/routing problem, not a regional one. A national fix, not a local pilot.
OPS
03
Win back 69,739 lost customers with real reach
7× more customers are gone-for-good than recovered. A small nudge won't work at this scale — needs a strong, trust-focused incentive and broad reach.
GROWTH
04
Enforce restaurant quality, not just re-listing
Only 1,266 of 9,102 "recovered" restaurants are actually premium. The network came back in quantity, not quality — same underlying trust gap.
PARTNERS
05
Track CAC separately — paid growth isn't real recovery
Paid channel share nearly doubled (20%→34%) with no proven order-value benefit (F=1.43, p=0.23). Recovery funded entirely by paid spend isn't a brand recovery.
CAC
06
Set hard monthly recovery targets
Three months flat at ~8,200 orders. Without explicit targets — order growth, rating floor, SLA compliance — the recovery budget gets absorbed without structural change.
