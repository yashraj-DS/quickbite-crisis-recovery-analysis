# 🍔 QuickBite Express: Crisis Recovery Analysis

## Project Overview

QuickBite Express is a Bengaluru-based food delivery startup that experienced a major business disruption in June 2025 due to a food safety incident and a week-long delivery outage. These events led to a sharp decline in customer orders, lower satisfaction ratings, increased acquisition costs, and the loss of several restaurant partners.

This project analyzes the impact of the crisis across three business phases—Pre-Crisis, Crisis, and Recovery—using Python, MySQL, and Power BI. The objective is to uncover key business insights, evaluate recovery progress, and provide actionable recommendations to support management decisions.

---

## Business Objectives

- Analyze customer behavior before, during, and after the crisis.
- Understand changes in order patterns across different phases.
- Evaluate delivery performance and operational efficiency.
- Assess customer ratings and sentiment trends.
- Identify valuable restaurant partnerships.
- Build an interactive dashboard for executive decision-making.
